// PROJECT ID: 003 — GENERATIVE AI KNOWLEDGE ASSISTANT

GEN AI FAQ // BUSINESS VALUE

Give your customers instant, accurate answers around the clock — an FAQ that understands meaning, not just keywords.

Fine-tuned Encoder RAG LLM

The Problem

Static FAQ pages rely on the visitor typing the right keyword. A customer service team fields the same repetitive questions all day, in more than one language, and a badly-phrased search finds nothing even when the answer already exists.

The Solution

A semantic search engine, fine-tuned specifically on the FAQ's own bilingual (FR/EN) content: every answer is encoded once, each incoming question is encoded on the fly, and the two are ranked by similarity — finding the right answer even when it's poorly phrased, with a visible confidence score, and an optional LLM step to generate a natural-language reply from the retrieved answer. When nothing matches with enough confidence, it says so rather than inventing one.

Measured Value

  • No customer data sent to a third party for search.
  • Reduces repetitive support tickets — 24/7 availability.
  • No per-query variable cost — retrieval runs on a self-hosted encoder.
  • One engine covers both French and English.
  • Fine-tuning is measured, not assumed: MRR and Recall@1/3/5 are compared before vs. after, globally and per language.

Who It's For

E-commerce support (shipping, returns), ticketing platforms (exchanges, refunds, access), practical-information FAQs (hours, pricing, seasonal conditions), B2B SaaS self-service support, and multilingual travel/hospitality guest FAQs — anywhere repetitive, well-documented questions currently reach a human first.

// PROJECT ID: 001 — SAAS RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM

LIKYLY // BUSINESS VALUE

Help your users discover what they'll love next — recommendations that adapt to real behavior, not a static rule sheet.

Embeddings Machine Learning (ALS) Recommendation Systems

The Problem

Most catalogs still show the same "related items" to every visitor — a rule set that is configured once and never updated with what people actually click, view or buy. A new visitor, a new product, or a returning user with a full basket all see the exact same shelf.

The Solution

Likyly combines three recommendation strategies into one hybrid engine:

  • Content-based — TF-IDF plus sentence-transformer embeddings capture thematic similarity from product text, computed the moment a product is ingested, so it's recommendable immediately (no waiting for a retraining cycle).
  • Collaborative — implicit ALS trained on real interactions, weighting a purchase more than a view, with no fabricated ratings.
  • Weighted hybrid — content and collaborative scores blend through a tunable parameter, adjustable per request, and it works from the first pageview — with or without an account.

Why Not Just Call an LLM per Request?

It's tempting to ask a general-purpose LLM "what should this user see next?" on every request. Likyly deliberately doesn't: specialized models (TF-IDF, embeddings, ALS), tracked and versioned with MLflow, are precomputed or resolve in a few tens of milliseconds — free to run, fully private (self-hosted), and deterministic. A generic LLM call is billed per request, adds seconds of latency at e-commerce traffic scale, and depends on a third-party provider for a decision that needs to be instant and explainable.

Who It's For

SaaS products, e-commerce catalogs and content platforms (products, films, articles, cultural content) that want real personalization without shipping user or catalog data to a third-party LLM on every request. Ships as Python, JS/TS and PHP SDKs.
